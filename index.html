Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about tyre care, wheel alignment, balancing, and visiting Yamoto near Gachibowli.

How do I know if my car needs a wheel alignment?

If your car pulls to one side, your steering wheel is off-centre, or your tyres wear unevenly, get a wheel alignment checked. Yamoto in Narsingi offers precision wheel alignment services.

Can hitting a pothole damage my wheel alignment?

Yes. Potholes and kerbs can knock your wheels out of alignment. If you notice pulling or vibrations after an impact, get your alignment checked.

Why does my steering wheel shake at high speed?

This is often caused by unbalanced wheels. Tyre damage or a bent wheel can also cause it. Yamoto offers wheel balancing to help resolve speed-related vibrations.

How often should I get my wheels balanced?

Get wheels balanced when fitting new tyres, after a strong pothole impact, or if you feel vibrations. As a general guide, balancing every 5,000–8,000 km is recommended.

What is the difference between wheel alignment and wheel balancing?

Alignment adjusts wheel angles so your car drives straight. Balancing corrects uneven weight in the tyre and wheel assembly to prevent vibrations. Yamoto offers both services.

How do I find my car’s tyre size?

Check the code on your tyre sidewall, such as 205/55 R16. You can also find it on the driver’s door sticker or in your car manual.
