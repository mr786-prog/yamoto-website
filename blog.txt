7 Signs It’s Time to Replace Your Car Tyres
Your tyres are the only part of your car touching the road. Worn ones affect braking, grip and safety, especially on wet roads. Here’s what to look for.
1. Worn tread
Look for small raised bars inside the tyre grooves, called tread wear indicators. When the tread is level with them (about 1.6 mm), it’s time for new tyres.
2. Cracks on the sidewall
Fine cracks on the side of the tyre mean the rubber is drying out, often from heat and age.
3. Bulges or bubbles
A bulge means the tyre’s inner structure is damaged. Stop driving on it and replace it.
4. Constant vibration
Shaking at speed can mean an unbalanced wheel, but it can also point to a damaged or unevenly worn tyre. Get it checked.
5. Frequent pressure loss
If a tyre keeps going flat or needs topping up, it may have a slow leak or too many repairs.
6. Age
Find the four-digit date code on the sidewall. For example, 2423 means week 24 of 2023. Tyres age even when unused, so have anything over five years old inspected.
7. Poor grip in the rain
If your car skids or takes longer to stop on wet roads, the grip is fading.
