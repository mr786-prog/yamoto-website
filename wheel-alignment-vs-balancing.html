Wheel Alignment vs Wheel Balancing: Which One Does Your Car Need?

Alignment fixes pulling and uneven wear whereas balancing fixes vibration. Learn which your car needs, from Yamoto in Narsingi, Hyderabad.

By Yamoto Technical Team
•
⏱ 3 min read

Many drivers use these two terms as if they mean the same thing. They don’t. Each fixes a different problem, and knowing which you need saves time and money.

What is wheel alignment?

Alignment adjusts the angle of your wheels so they point straight and sit properly on the road, as set by your car’s manufacturer. It fixes pulling to one side and uneven tyre wear.

What is wheel balancing?

Balancing makes sure weight is spread evenly around each wheel and tyre. Small weights are added where needed. It fixes vibration, especially at higher speeds.

Which one do you need?

Quick Diagnostic Guide

Symptom & Fix
What you notice Likely fix
Car pulls to one side Alignment
Steering wheel crooked when driving straight Alignment
One edge of a tyre wearing faster Alignment
Steering wheel or seat vibrates at speed Balancing
Shaking that gets worse as you go faster Balancing

Do you need both?

Often, yes. It’s a good idea to have both done when you fit new tyres, so they wear evenly and ride smoothly from the start. After hitting a deep pothole, alignment is usually the first thing to check.
