8 Simple Habits to Make Your Tyres Last Longer in Hyderabad

Hyderabad is a great city to drive in, but its roads are hard on cars. Potholes, speed breakers, uneven patches and monsoon damage all put stress on your wheels and tyres. You can’t change the roads, but you can change how you deal with them. Here are eight habits that help.

Habit 01

1. Slow down for speed breakers

Hitting a speed breaker at speed sends a hard jolt through the wheel, tyre and suspension. Slow down, and ease off the brake just before the bump rather than during it.

Habit 02

2. Brake before a pothole, don’t swerve

Swerving at the last second is risky and can still clip the edge. Brake smoothly in advance when it’s safe, and roll through slowly if you can’t avoid it.

Habit 03

3. Don’t hit the kerb

Scraping or bumping a kerb while parking is a common cause of misaligned wheels and sidewall damage. Take your time on tight lanes and parking spots.

Habit 04

4. Check tyre pressure regularly

Heat and rough roads change pressure faster than you’d expect. Too low or too high wears tyres unevenly and makes them more likely to be damaged by potholes. Check it about once a month, with the tyres cold, and use the figure in your owner’s manual or on the door-jamb sticker.

Habit 05

5. Take extra care in the monsoon

Water hides potholes, and broken roads after heavy rain are hard on wheels. Drive slowly through standing water, and get your tyres and alignment checked once the rains ease.

Habit 06

6. Don’t overload the car

Extra weight puts more stress on tyres, especially on rough roads. Stay within your car’s recommended load.

Habit 07

7. Watch for early warning signs

Pulling to one side, a crooked steering wheel, vibration or uneven tyre wear are all signs something has shifted. Don’t wait for it to get worse. The fix is usually cheaper the earlier you catch it. See our guides on alignment vs balancing and signs your tyres need replacing.

Habit 08

8. Get a regular check-up

Even careful drivers can’t avoid every bump. A regular alignment and balancing check, plus rotating your tyres, keeps wear even and helps your tyres last longer.
